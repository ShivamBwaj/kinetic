Questions

Is Kinetic free?

Yes. Every template, style and export format is free, with no account, and your first 3 exports each month are clean. After that, exports carry a small “Made with Kinetic” badge in the corner until the next month. Kinetic Pro gives unlimited clean exports for a one-time payment: $9 for the first 100 buyers, then $19.

How does Pro work?

Buy once and you get a licence key on the next page and by email. Click Activate in Kinetic on that page, or paste the key under Get Pro in the app. One key works on 3 PCs and never expires. Refunds and revocations are handled by Dodo Payments, our payment provider.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The built-in writer, the editor and rendering all work offline. The internet is only used if you pull a brand from a URL or have the AI write your film.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes. Kinetic runs in your browser on Mac, Windows, Linux and ChromeOS: sign in with Google and start. There is also a desktop app for Windows 10 and 11.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the download against its SHA-256: {{SHA256}}

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. The videos you export are yours, and the built-in music is synthesised in the app, so it is licence-free.

Do my screenshots or prompts get uploaded?

Your screenshots and videos never leave your PC; films render locally. When the AI writes a film, your one-sentence description goes to Kinetic AI (and on to the model provider) to write the script, and descriptions typed on this website are logged so we can see what people try. With your own AI key, it goes to that provider instead. With the built-in offline writer, nothing leaves your PC at all.
