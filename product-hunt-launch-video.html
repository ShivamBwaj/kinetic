What a good Product Hunt video does

People scroll past dozens of launches a day. A short film in the first gallery slot has a few seconds to make them stop, so the order matters:

  1. Hook in the first two seconds. The pain or the price, in big type, before your logo.
  2. Show the real product. Your actual screens, close up, not a stock laptop.
  3. One idea per scene. Three features shown well beat ten listed.
  4. End on the offer. Your URL and what's free, held long enough to read.

Kinetic's launch templates are built in exactly this shape, with cuts timed to the music, so you start from a film that already works and only change the words.

How to add it to your launch

  1. Export a 16:9 MP4 from Kinetic.
  2. Upload it to YouTube and make sure it is not private.
  3. Add the full YouTube URL to your Product Hunt launch's video field. Preview the gallery to confirm it loads.
  4. For launch-day social posts, export a separate 9:16 vertical launch video.

Product Hunt currently supports YouTube links for the video field. Check its official posting instructions before publishing. Verified 31 October 2026.

Why not an agency or After Effects?

Questions

How long should a Product Hunt video be?

Use enough time to show the problem, the product and the next action clearly. Preview a Kinetic launch template to see its actual duration, then add, remove or retime scenes for your story. There is no measured conversion-optimal duration claimed here.

Can I upload the video to Product Hunt directly?

The Product Hunt video field currently accepts a full YouTube link. Export an MP4 from Kinetic, upload it to YouTube, confirm it is not private and paste the full link into your launch.
