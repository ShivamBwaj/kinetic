From a sentence to an MP4.

No timeline to learn first. You start with a finished film and change what you want.

  1. Describe it

    Say what you're launching, paste your URL to pull your name and colours, drop in screenshots. Or start from a template.

  2. Edit anything

    Click a scene to change its words, colours, length or transition. Drag scenes to reorder. Undo everything.

  3. Export

    Render MP4 or GIF on your PC in 16:9 for YouTube and X, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, or 1:1 for feeds.

Questions

Is Kinetic free?

Yes. Every template, style and export format is free, with no account. Free exports carry a small “Made with Kinetic” badge in the corner. Kinetic Pro removes it for a one-time $19.

How does Pro work?

Buy once and you get a licence key on the next page and by email. Click Activate in Kinetic on that page, or paste the key under Get Pro in the app. One key works on 3 PCs and never expires. Refunds and revocations are handled by Dodo Payments, our payment provider.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The built-in writer, the editor and rendering all work offline. The internet is only used if you pull a brand from a URL or use an AI key.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Kinetic runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the download against its SHA-256: cc45f1f5adf65c0be72a9643da83cde4a4620607c2be7dc96d3d72c86d0fab89

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. The videos you export are yours, and the built-in music is synthesised in the app, so it is licence-free.

Do my screenshots or prompts get uploaded?

Not by Kinetic. Your files stay on your PC. If you add an AI key, your prompt and the text of your scenes go to that provider to write the script.
