Built for software, not stock footage

Most video makers are built around stock clips and talking heads. SaaS launches need something else: your interface, shown clearly, with the one thing it changes for the user. Kinetic's scene library is made of exactly those pieces: browser frames, laptop shots, UI close-ups that zoom into a button, toggles, notifications, chat bubbles, charts, pricing cards and counters.

Every scene is editable text and images, so updating the film for the next release takes minutes, not a new brief.

Questions

Do I need screenshots?

They help, but no. Without them Kinetic uses a clean mock interface in your colours, so you can make a teaser before the product is finished.

Can I make a feature announcement instead of a full launch?

Yes. The Feature drop template shows one new feature on your real UI: a close-up, a click and the result.

Where does rendering happen?

On your PC. Your screenshots and projects never leave your computer.
