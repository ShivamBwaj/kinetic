Privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026.

The app

Kinetic runs on your PC. Your prompts, screenshots, projects and exported videos are stored on your computer and are not sent to us. There is no account and no analytics in the app.

When the app uses the internet

  • Pull brand from URL: Kinetic opens the page you enter to read its name, colours, text and screenshots.
  • AI keys: if you add a DeepSeek, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Anthropic key, your prompt and scene text are sent to that provider under its own privacy policy. Keys are stored encrypted on your PC.
  • Claude Code or Codex: if you choose a local agent, it runs on your PC with your own login.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no cookies, trackers or analytics. GitHub may log requests as described in its privacy statement. Downloads are served by GitHub Releases.
