Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026.

The app

Kinetic runs on your PC. Your screenshots, projects and exported videos are stored on your computer and are not sent to us. There is no account. The app sends a few anonymous usage pings so we know it works for people: that it was opened for the first time, that a video was exported (its aspect ratio and whether it carried the free badge), that Pro was activated, and that the buy button was clicked. A ping contains the event, the app version and the approximate city and country derived from your connection. It carries no name, email, file content, prompt or device ID, and IP addresses are not stored.

When the app uses the internet

  • Pull brand from URL: Kinetic opens the page you enter to read its name, colours, text and screenshots.
  • Kinetic AI: when Kinetic AI writes a film (in the app or in the "try it" box on this website), your product description goes to our server and on to the AI model provider (via OpenRouter) to write the script. Descriptions typed on this website are logged, with the approximate city and country, so we can see what people try and how much the AI costs. A random ID stored in your browser or app counts your free films.
  • AI keys: if you add a DeepSeek, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Anthropic key, your prompt and scene text are sent to that provider under its own privacy policy. Keys are stored encrypted on your PC.
  • Claude Code or Codex: if you choose a local agent, it runs on your PC with your own login.

This website

This site is hosted on Vercel and uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without storing IP addresses. When you download the installer or click a buy button, we receive the same kind of anonymous event as above (event and approximate city and country). Downloads are served by GitHub Releases, which may log requests as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

To measure useful links, this website also counts internal-link, download-button and checkout-button clicks by public page path. A session-only browser storage value remembers the first page path for this visit. These aggregate counters contain no names, emails, prompts, query strings or visitor IDs and expire after 120 days. IP addresses are used temporarily in memory to suppress repeated identical events and are not stored with the counters. A checkout click is not proof of a completed purchase.
