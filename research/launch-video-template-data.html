How long are Kinetic's video templates?

We measured every template in Kinetic 0.4.0, using the same timeline planner that drives preview and export. Here are the lengths, scene counts and starting formats.

Download the CSVPreview the templates

The findings

  • The median planned video length across 40 Kinetic templates is 13.4 seconds.
  • The shortest planned template is 7.3 seconds; the longest is 20.3 seconds.
  • 31 of 40 templates start in 16:9 format.
  • 3 of 40 templates start in 9:16 format.
  • 6 of 40 templates start in 1:1 format.

These describe Kinetic's own library. They are not a sample of all launch videos, user exports, render-speed benchmarks or evidence of which length converts best. You can retime a template and export it in another shape.

How we measured

The build loads every template in this release, applies the same fictional sample brand, and runs the app's timeline planner at the template's native aspect ratio. The reported duration includes the planner's half-beat end tail. The planner accounts for each scene's beat count, tempo and pace setting. Values in the dataset retain three decimal places; summary figures are rounded to one.
