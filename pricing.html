How the founding offer works

The first 100 Pro buyers pay $9 once. After those licences sell, Pro costs $19 once. The Pro checkout selects the applicable offer. Check the final amount and any applicable tax at checkout before paying.

What happens after payment?

Dodo Payments handles checkout. Your licence key appears after payment and arrives by email. Open Get Pro in Kinetic and activate the key. Pro has no recurring subscription.

Can I try the finished export first?

Yes. Download the free app and make a film with your own screenshots. Your first 3 exports each calendar month have no Kinetic badge. Free editing continues after that allowance is used.
