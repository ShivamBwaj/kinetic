Kinetic / Launch video guide

Make a launch video from your screenshots.

A practical workflow for a founder making a software launch film in Kinetic: show one problem, one useful result and one next step.

This guide covers Kinetic's screenshot-based workflow. For product details and contact information, meet Kinetic and its maker.

1. Choose the viewer and the action

Write a brief in one sentence: “For [buyer], show how [product] solves [problem], then ask them to [action].” Keep the call to action consistent with your landing page.

2. Capture the screens that prove the result

Use a screen showing the starting problem and one showing the completed task. Hide private customer data and make the important labels readable. Keep crops consistent. Use real screenshots for product claims; a mock interface is useful for a teaser but should not imply a finished feature.

3. Start from an editable film

Install Kinetic on Windows 10 or 11, open Templates and choose SaaS launch or Product demo. Add your product name, URL and screenshots. Edit the words and scenes to fit your offer. Choose a visual style that keeps your interface readable.

4. Give each scene one job

Here is an illustrative storyboard for a fictional feedback app. Replace the sample copy with facts your product can demonstrate.

  1. Problem: “Feedback in five different places.” Show the starting screen.
  2. Product: “Meet Nova.” Show the real dashboard.
  3. Result: “One queue for your team's feedback.” Show the consolidated queue, with a close-up of the relevant control.
  4. Next action: “Try Nova” and your URL. Keep the end card on screen long enough to read.

Watch once muted to check the story, then with music to check the cuts. Shorten any line you cannot comfortably read before the next scene. Treat the video as a preview; use a screen recorder separately if buyers need to see a continuous live workflow.

5. Preview each shape before you export

Use 16:9 for a website player, 9:16 for a vertical feed, or 1:1 for a square post. Kinetic can re-layout the film, but preview each version to check screenshot crops and text placement. Export MP4 for a video player or GIF for a short silent loop. Check the destination's current upload requirements.
