About Kinetic

Kinetic is a LaunchLoop product made by Shivam. It helps founders turn their product's words and screenshots into an editable motion launch video.

What the app does

Start with a prompt, a website URL or a template. Change the words, colours, screenshots and scene timing, then render MP4 or GIF on your Windows PC. Browse 40 templates and 17 styles to see the available looks.

What to know before installing

Kinetic currently runs on Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. It exports animated scenes and screenshots; it does not include screen recording or an After Effects project export. The Windows installer is not code-signed yet. Find the installer and release details on the official GitHub releases page.

Local rendering and optional AI

The offline writer, editing and video rendering work on your PC. Importing a brand from a URL and using a hosted AI provider require the internet. See the privacy policy for what is sent and collected.
