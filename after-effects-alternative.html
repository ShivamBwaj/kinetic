Where Kinetic fits

Use After Effects when you need a one-off piece with custom illustration, 3D or character animation, or you already work as a motion designer.

Use Kinetic when you need a launch film, product demo, feature drop or social cut this week, and you'd rather edit text than keyframes. Kinetic ships 38 scene types, 12 visual styles and 40 templates, all timed to music, and renders on your PC.

What you don't have to do

  • No keyframes, easing curves or pre-comps.
  • No plugin hunting for kinetic type.
  • No re-animating when the copy changes: edit the line and the scene re-times itself.
  • No separate edits for vertical and square: switch the format and export again.

Questions

Can I export to After Effects or Premiere?

Kinetic exports MP4 and GIF. You can bring the MP4 into any editor to combine it with other footage.

Is it really free?

Yes, with 3 clean exports a month; later exports that month carry a small badge. Pro is a one-time $19 for unlimited clean exports.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. Kinetic renders frame by frame on your PC; a faster machine just finishes sooner. There is also a draft mode for quick previews.
