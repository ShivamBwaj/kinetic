What goes into an app promo

A promo has one job: make someone think “I want that”. Start with the moment your app fixes, show the screen that fixes it, then show it fitting into a day. Kinetic's social and launch templates follow that arc and keep each scene to one idea, so the message survives a muted autoplay.
